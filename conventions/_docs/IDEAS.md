# IDEAS

## Saving an idea

- Record an idea only at the user's explicit request: when they identify a proposal as an idea or ask to save it as one. Do not turn ordinary requests, research, comparisons, or recommendations into idea records automatically.
- Store each idea awaiting review in `wiki/ideas/YYYY_MM_DD_IDEA_TITLE.md`. Use the date it was first saved and keep the index current. Preserve existing names and dates.

## Transferring an idea

1. Follow the agreed destination's procedure. Saving a proposal or transferring it to a roadmap or tracker does not approve it or authorize implementation.
2. Read the destination and verify that it preserves the idea, context, links, and constraints. If it is inaccessible or incomplete, keep the source and report what is missing.
3. Once verified, remove the original using the [wiki deletion procedure](./WIKI.md#moves-and-deletions) and point editable incoming links to the destination. Do not leave a redirect or transfer other ideas without a separate request.
